Introduction

The as-purchased design of the pants were restrictively shallow. Sitting with a phone in the front pocket lead to pressure on the hip and stress against the pocket lining. Using available materials, I was able to increase the pocket capacity three fold.

Process

The first (right) pocket was modified in a more freeform way.

The second (left) pocket was modified in a more methodical way, using strategies learned from modifying the first pocket. Initial planning allowed for the second pocket to begin in a neater fashion, leading to compounding improvements and superior final result. First, the intended pattern was measured and traced onto paper.


Then, that template was used to mark and cut the fabric.


Once cut, it could be folded and ironed.


And then machine sewn and inverted. This inversion protects the edges during machine washes. Additionally, it results in a cleaner and more comfortable lining.


Final attachment to the rest of the pants was done by hand.
